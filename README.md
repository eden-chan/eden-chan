# [Eden Chan](https://edenchan.ca)

I enjoy finding better shower thoughts and hills to climb.

It feels great to deeply understand things that bring me joy. These tend to be incompressible problems with high-dimensional people. Vast, brilliant, and impossible to fully understand.

I love working with awesome people and gaining a deeper understanding of the [universe of things I care about](https://edenchan.ca/universe).

I joined [xAI](https://x.ai) on the talent engineering team. I wanted to learn how mafias are born and raised. The best engineers are both exceptional problem solvers and problem finders. The taste of choosing the right problems to solve and framing it in the right light is irreplaceable.

Before that, I grew up in Toronto, graduated from [Waterloo](https://x.com/edenchan/status/1935560720239964192), lived with my best friends in a hacker house in San Francisco, Canggu & Kyoto, and now Palo Alto. During that time, I worked at [Alljoined](https://alljoined.com), [Ideaflow](https://ideaflow.app/), [Bronco](https://bronco.ai).
